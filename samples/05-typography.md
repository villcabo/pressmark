## A long paragraph

Deduplication exists but did not work, and the reason is in a detail that looks
minor until you look closely: the hash meant to stop the duplicate is computed
over a field the bank does not always send. In the first notification it
arrived empty and in the second with a value, so the hashes differed and the
uniqueness constraint never had anything to catch. The protection was not
badly written; it was looking in the wrong place.
